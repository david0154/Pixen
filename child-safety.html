Pixen Child Safety Standards Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Pixen is committed to providing a safe environment for all users, especially children and young people. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse, exploitation, grooming, and any content or behavior that endangers minors.

1. Our Commitment

Pixen strictly prohibits:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)
  • Child exploitation in any form
  • Child grooming
  • Sexualization of minors
  • Human trafficking involving minors
  • Bullying, harassment, or abuse of children
  • Any activity that threatens the safety or well-being of minors

Any violation of this policy may result in immediate content removal, account suspension, permanent account termination, and reporting to relevant law enforcement authorities.

2. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users can report content, accounts, messages, or activities that may involve child exploitation or abuse. Reports can be submitted through:

  • In-app reporting tools
  • Email support

Child Safety Contact Email: pixen@nexuzy.in

Reports are reviewed as quickly as possible by our moderation team.

3. Detection and Enforcement

Pixen may use a combination of user reports, automated moderation systems, and human review processes to identify and remove content that violates child safety standards.

We reserve the right to:

  • Remove content without notice
  • Restrict user access
  • Permanently ban offending accounts
  • Preserve evidence when legally required
  • Cooperate with law enforcement agencies

4. Age Requirements

Users must be at least 13 years old, or the minimum age required by local law, to use Pixen. If we discover that a user does not meet the minimum age requirement, we may suspend or delete the account and associated data.

5. Cooperation with Authorities

Pixen cooperates with relevant law enforcement agencies and child protection organizations when required by law. Where legally required, reports involving child exploitation, abuse, or CSAM may be referred to appropriate authorities.

6. User Responsibilities

Users must:

  • Respect the safety and privacy of minors.
  • Never upload, share, request, or distribute exploitative content.
  • Report suspicious activity involving children immediately.
  • Comply with all applicable laws and Pixen policies.

7. Educational and Preventive Measures

Pixen supports efforts to:

  • Prevent online child exploitation.
  • Promote responsible digital behavior.
  • Improve platform safety through moderation and reporting tools.

8. Policy Violations

Violations of this Child Safety Standards Policy may result in:

  • Content removal
  • Account restrictions
  • Permanent account termination
  • Reporting to law enforcement
  • Legal action where applicable

9. Contact Information

For child safety concerns, abuse reports, or policy questions:

Pixen Child Safety Team
Email: pixen@nexuzy.in

Pixen is committed to maintaining a safe platform and protecting children from abuse, exploitation, and harmful content.
