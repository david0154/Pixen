Pixen Community Guidelines

Last Updated: June 2026

Welcome to Pixen. Our goal is to create a safe, positive, and respectful community where people can share photos, videos, stories, and connect with others. By using Pixen, you agree to follow these Community Guidelines.

1. Respect Others

Treat everyone with respect. Do not:

  • Harass, threaten, or intimidate others.
  • Use abusive, hateful, or discriminatory language.
  • Engage in bullying or targeted harassment.
  • Encourage violence against individuals or groups.

2. Share Authentic Content

Be genuine and honest. Do not:

  • Impersonate another person or organization.
  • Create fake accounts for deceptive purposes.
  • Spread misinformation intended to harm others.
  • Manipulate engagement through spam or fake activity.

3. Keep Content Safe and Legal

You may not post content that:

  • Violates any law or regulation.
  • Promotes criminal activity.
  • Contains malware, viruses, or harmful code.
  • Encourages dangerous or harmful behavior.

4. Child Safety

Pixen has a zero-tolerance policy toward:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)
  • Child exploitation
  • Child grooming
  • Sexualization of minors
  • Abuse involving children

Any such content or behavior will result in immediate account suspension and may be reported to law enforcement authorities.

5. Nudity and Sexual Content

Do not post:

  • Explicit sexual content
  • Pornographic material
  • Sexual exploitation content
  • Non-consensual intimate imagery

Limited artistic, educational, or awareness content may be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

6. Violence and Harmful Content

Do not post content that:

  • Promotes violence or terrorism
  • Glorifies self-harm or suicide
  • Encourages dangerous challenges
  • Shows graphic violence intended to shock or harm viewers

7. Hate Speech and Discrimination

Pixen does not allow content that attacks or promotes hatred against people based on:

  • Race
  • Ethnicity
  • Nationality
  • Religion
  • Disability
  • Gender
  • Sexual orientation
  • Age

8. Privacy and Personal Information

Respect the privacy of others. Do not share:

  • Personal phone numbers without consent
  • Home addresses
  • Government identification documents
  • Financial information
  • Private conversations without permission

9. Intellectual Property

Only share content you own or have permission to use. Do not:

  • Upload copyrighted content without authorization.
  • Infringe trademarks or intellectual property rights.
  • Distribute stolen content.

10. Spam and Platform Abuse

The following activities are prohibited:

  • Unwanted advertising
  • Mass messaging
  • Automated account creation
  • Artificial engagement manipulation
  • Scams and phishing attempts

11. Reporting Violations

If you see content or behavior that violates these guidelines, please use the reporting tools available in Pixen or contact:

Email: pixen@nexuzy.in

Our moderation team will review reports and take appropriate action.

12. Enforcement Actions

Pixen may take the following actions when guidelines are violated:

  • Content removal
  • Warning notices
  • Temporary restrictions
  • Account suspension
  • Permanent account termination
  • Reporting to law enforcement when required

13. Changes to These Guidelines

We may update these Community Guidelines from time to time. Continued use of Pixen after updates means you accept the revised guidelines.

14. Contact Us

For questions about these Community Guidelines:

Pixen Community Team
Email: pixen@nexuzy.in
Developer: Nexuzy Lab

Thank you for helping keep Pixen safe, respectful, and welcoming for everyone.
