Pixen Account Deletion Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Pixen respects users’ rights to control their personal information. This Account Deletion Policy explains how users can request deletion of their Pixen account and associated data.

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1: Through the App

  1. Open the Pixen app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Select Account Settings.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

Option 2: By Email

If you cannot access your account, send a deletion request to:

Email: pixen@nexuzy.in

Please include:

  • Your registered email address or phone number.
  • Your Pixen username.
  • A brief request for account deletion.

What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon account deletion, the following information will be permanently removed from Pixen systems:

  • Profile information
  • Photos and videos uploaded by you
  • Posts and stories
  • Comments and reactions
  • Messages (where technically feasible)
  • Account preferences
  • Authentication information

Data Retention

Certain information may be retained for a limited period when required for:

  • Legal compliance
  • Fraud prevention
  • Security investigations
  • Enforcement of Terms & Conditions

Any retained information will be securely stored and deleted when no longer required.

Deletion Processing Time

Most deletion requests are processed within 30 days of verification.

Effect of Account Deletion

Once your account is deleted:

  • Your profile will no longer be visible.
  • You will lose access to your account.
  • Deleted data generally cannot be recovered.
  • Some content shared with other users may remain visible if required for system integrity.

Contact Us

For questions regarding account deletion:

Pixen Support
Email: pixen@nexuzy.in
Developer: Nexuzy Lab

By using Pixen, you acknowledge and agree to this Account Deletion Policy.
