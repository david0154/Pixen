Pixen Account Deletion Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Account deletion request: You can request permanent deletion of your Pixen account and associated data through the app or by contacting support.

Pixen respects users’ rights to control their personal information. This page explains how to request deletion of your account, what data may be removed, and what may be retained for legal or security reasons.

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1: Through the App

  1. Open the Pixen app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Select Account Settings.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

Option 2: By Email

If you cannot access your account, send a deletion request to:

Email: pixen@nexuzy.in

Please include your registered email address or phone number, your Pixen username, and a short deletion request.

What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon account deletion, the following information may be permanently removed from Pixen systems:

  • Profile information
  • Photos and videos uploaded by you
  • Posts and stories
  • Comments and reactions
  • Messages, where technically feasible
  • Account preferences
  • Authentication information

Data Retention

Certain information may be retained for a limited period when required for legal compliance, fraud prevention, security investigations, or enforcement of Terms & Conditions. Any retained information will be securely stored and deleted when no longer required.

Deletion Processing Time

Processing time: Most verified deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

Effect of Account Deletion

  • Your profile will no longer be visible.
  • You will lose access to your account.
  • Deleted data generally cannot be recovered.
  • Some content shared with other users may remain visible if required for system integrity or legal reasons.
