Pixen Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Pixen (“we,” “our,” or “us”), operated by Nexuzy Lab, respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Pixen mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create a Pixen account, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Username
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Profile photo
  • Date of birth (if provided)

User Content

We collect content you create and share, including:

  • Photos
  • Videos
  • Comments
  • Messages
  • Stories
  • Profile information

Device Information

We may collect:

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Device identifiers
  • Language settings

Location Information

With your permission, Pixen may collect precise or approximate location information to provide location-based features and improve user experience.

Usage Information

We may collect information about how you interact with Pixen, including:

  • Login activity
  • Content interactions
  • Search activity
  • Feature usage
  • Crash reports and diagnostics

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain Pixen services
  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Deliver social networking features
  • Improve app performance and security
  • Detect fraud, spam, and abuse
  • Personalize user experience
  • Send service-related notifications
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information. Security measures include:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure cloud storage
  • Access controls
  • Monitoring and abuse prevention systems

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

4. Data Sharing

Pixen does not sell personal information to third parties. We may share information only:

  • With service providers necessary for app operation
  • When required by law
  • To protect the rights, safety, and security of users
  • During business transfers or legal restructuring

5. Firebase and Third-Party Services

Pixen may use services such as:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Storage
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Analytics services

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

6. User Communications

Messages and content shared through Pixen may be processed and stored to provide platform functionality and maintain service integrity.

7. Children’s Privacy

Pixen is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will remove it promptly.

8. Child Safety and Protection

Pixen maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Child exploitation
  • Grooming
  • Abuse of minors

Content violating these policies may be removed immediately, and accounts may be permanently suspended and reported to appropriate authorities.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and data
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

10. Account Deletion

Users can request deletion of their account and associated data through the app settings or by contacting:

Email: pixen@nexuzy.in

We will process deletion requests within a reasonable period, subject to legal obligations.

11. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary to:

  • Provide services
  • Meet legal requirements
  • Resolve disputes
  • Enforce our agreements

After retention periods expire, data is deleted or anonymized.

12. International Data Transfers

Your information may be stored and processed on servers located in different countries where our service providers operate.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted within the application and on our website.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact:

Pixen Support
Email: pixen@nexuzy.in
Developer: Nexuzy Lab

By using Pixen, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
